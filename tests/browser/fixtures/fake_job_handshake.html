Job Description

Student will be responsible for setting up and maintaining equipment in the QI Makerspace, including but not limited to 3D printers, laser cutters, CNC routers, traditional wood and metal shop tools, electronics equipment, and computer workstations.

Job Responsibilities

  • Mentor and supervise users of the makerspace

Qualifications

  • Skills in a variety of fabrication tools and practices
  • Ability to mentor and supervise fellow students
