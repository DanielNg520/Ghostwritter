Warehouse Associate

Initech Logistics LLC

Job description

About this role: Initech Logistics LLC is looking for a reliable Warehouse Associate to join our growing distribution team. This is a fast-paced environment where attention to detail and safety matter.

Job Responsibilities

  • Load and unload trucks safely and efficiently
  • Operate forklifts and other warehouse equipment
  • Maintain accurate inventory records
  • Follow all safety protocols and procedures

Requirements

  • Ability to lift up to 50 lbs repeatedly throughout a shift
  • Forklift certification preferred but not required
  • Prior warehouse experience is a plus

Preferred qualifications

High school diploma or equivalent. Experience with inventory management systems is a plus.

Who you are

You care about safety, teamwork, and getting the job done right every single day.
